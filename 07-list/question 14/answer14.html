Matriculation
This refers to the formal process of enrolling or registering as a student at a college or university. Once you complete matriculation, you are officially recognized as a member of the institution and can attend classes, take exams, and access campus facilities.
Tenure
This is an employment status granted to experienced professors and academic staff that gives them permanent job security. Designed to protect academic freedom, tenure ensures that professors can research, teach, and express opinions on controversial topics without fear of being fired without just cause.
